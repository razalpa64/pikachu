RETRO ARCADE — CARTRIDGE NOTICES
================================

Every cartridge on the /retro shelf is a freely redistributable homebrew build
taken from a public repository. Nothing here is a commercial ROM, and none of
these files was downloaded from a ROM site. The manifest that ships to the
browser (public/retro/roms.json) carries the same source and licence links that
are listed below, and each player page links out to them.

The cover images in public/retro/covers/ are frames the emulator itself rendered
while the cartridge was running — screenshotted from the canvas at 2x and saved
at the Game Boy Advance's native 240x160. No artwork was drawn for this site.

YOUR OWN CARTRIDGE
------------------

/retro accepts a .gba file from your device, by file picker or by dropping it on
the "your own cartridge" card. That file is read by your browser for the 156-byte
cartridge header, handed to the emulator as an in-memory blob, and discarded when
the tab closes — it is never uploaded, never stored and never shared. Please only
load ROMs you are legally allowed to play; this site hosts none of them for you.


DOS SHELF
---------

The DOS player at /retro/dos.html runs js-dos 8.5.3 (GPL-2.0) with its own
DOSBox core, both served from public/retro/jsdos/. Five bundles ship in
public/retro/dos/bundles/:

  Calibration       Pikachu Arcade   CC0-1.0
                    A 179-byte test card written for this project: banner, four
                    colour bars, exit. No third-party code or data inside it.

  Celis             Grzegorz “GAD” Drozd   GPL-3.0
                    https://github.com/GadZombie/celis @ 01bea78
                    The author's own build and data from his repository; the
                    licence text is inside the bundle as LICENSE.TXT and the
                    corresponding source is that repository at that commit.

  Sadist            Grzegorz “GAD” Drozd   GPL-3.0
                    https://github.com/GadZombie/sadist @ 2372676
                    As above: binaries, data, artwork and Turbo Pascal source are
                    all published by the author under the GPL.

  Out of Cash       warrior-rockk   GPL-3.0
                    https://github.com/warrior-rockk/out-of-cash @ 8dd5c62
                    This is the project's own committed web/out-of-cash.jsdos,
                    served byte for byte and not repacked. Its source is the same
                    repository.

  Moth Hunter       Krzysztof Krystian Jankowski   MIT
                    https://github.com/w84death/x86-assembly @ 83a8662
                    A 508-byte .COM file from dos/game7/, packaged with the MIT
                    text as LICENSE.TXT — the notice that licence asks us to keep.

Three of those bundles were assembled by scripts/dos/make-games.ts from the
files at the commit recorded above and next to each game in the manifest; the
only thing this site adds is a dosbox.conf and a SOURCE.TXT note. Nothing was
compiled here, nothing was taken from a ROM site, and no commercial title is
hosted — Doom and Grand Theft Auto: Vice City in particular are absent because
both are still sold, their data files are not freely licensed, and Vice City was
never a DOS game.

Nothing else on the DOS shelf is committed to this repository. The discovery
index at public/retro/dos/catalog.json is a record of what another catalogue
contains, with each title's original source URL: the "Freeware" and "Shareware"
labels there describe how those games were sold, not who may redistribute them,
so those 1,499 records carry no download and no Play button. A title reaches the
shelf only after an administrator records the evidence for hosting it in the
database (site_content key dos.perm.<id>) and its .jsdos bundle is uploaded,
checked and run once.

A DOS game you own can be played by loading its .jsdos bundle through the admin
desk only — there is no visitor-facing "drop a file" box for DOS, because a
bundle is a whole installation and this site will not run one it has not checked.
